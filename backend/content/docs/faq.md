# Frequently Asked Questions

## Student

### How do I enroll in a course?

Open the course page and click **Enroll** or **Buy Now**. Paid courses require checkout completion.

### How do certificates work?

Complete all required lessons, quizzes, and projects per the course rules. Certificates appear under **Certificates** when earned.

### How do I submit a GitHub project?

Open the project lesson, click **Open Workspace**, paste your repository URL, and submit.

### Can I resume where I left off?

Yes. Learning Universe progress saves automatically. Reopen the course to continue from your last lesson.

## Instructor

### How do I create a quiz?

In Visual Studio, add a **Quiz** block. In Academic Studio, use `\quiz` with `\question` and `\option` commands.

### How do I publish a Learning Universe?

Complete your content in Visual or Academic Studio, then click **Publish**. Assets upload with the publish request.

### Academic vs Visual authoring?

Both produce identical `contentBlocks` JSON. Academic uses LaTeX DSL; Visual uses a drag-and-drop builder.

## Admin

### How do I enable AI features?

Go to **Admin → Settings → AI** and toggle features. Ensure API keys are configured on the server.

## Certificates

### What are certificate criteria?

Rules like "complete all lessons", "pass all quizzes", "minimum 70% score". Defined per course in authoring.

## Projects

### How are projects graded?

Instructors review submissions in **Project Reviews**, assign marks, and approve or reject.

## Payments

### What payment methods are supported?

The platform integrates with Razorpay/Stripe depending on deployment configuration.

## Courses

### Can I make a course free?

Set price to **0** in course or Learning Universe settings.

## Learning Universe

### What is a Learning Universe?

A structured learning path with tracks, modules, lessons, and ordered content blocks (theory, video, quiz, project, etc.).
