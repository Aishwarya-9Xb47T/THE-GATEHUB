# Getting Started with THE GATEHUB

Welcome to **THE GATEHUB** — your platform for structured learning, Learning Universes, certificates, and instructor-led courses.

> [!TIP] New here?
> Open the **AI Assistant** (⌘/) on any page and ask *"How do I get started?"* — it already knows you're in the Help Center.

## Logging In

To access your account:

1. Open THE GATEHUB in your browser.
2. Click **Login** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
3. Enter your registered **email address** and **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

> [!NOTE] First time?
> Click **Sign Up** on the landing page, choose Student or Instructor, complete registration, then log in.

> [!WARNING] Forgot your password?
> On the login page, click **Forgot Password**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. Links expire after 24 hours.

## Create an Account

1. Visit the [landing page](/) and click **Sign Up**.
2. Choose **Student** or **Instructor** during registration.
3. Verify your email if prompted, then log in.

> [!SUCCESS] You're ready
> After login you'll land on your role-specific dashboard — [Student Dashboard](/student) or [Instructor Dashboard](/instructor).

## Student Quick Start

- Browse courses from **[Dashboard → Browse](/student/browse)**.
- Enroll in free courses instantly; paid courses use secure checkout.
- Open **Learning Universes** for track-based structured paths.
- Track progress, quizzes, projects, and certificates from your dashboard.

| Action | Where to go |
|--------|-------------|
| Find courses | [Browse Courses](/student/browse) |
| My enrollments | [My Courses](/student/my-courses) |
| Certificates | [Certificates](/student/certificates) |
| Learning paths | [Learning Universe Guide](/help/learning-universe) |

## Instructor Quick Start

- Go to **[Create Course](/instructor/courses/new)** or **[Create Learning Universe](/instructor/learning-universe/new)**.
- Use **[Academic Authoring Studio](/instructor/learning-universe/new/academic)** (LaTeX) or **[Visual Authoring Studio](/instructor/learning-universe/new/visual)**.
- Publish content, set pricing, and review student project submissions.

> [!IMPORTANT] Before publishing
> Complete branding, curriculum, and at least one lesson. See the [Publishing Guide](/help/publishing) for the full checklist.

## Admin Quick Start

- Access **[Admin Dashboard](/admin)** for users, courses, payments, and analytics.
- Configure platform settings, AI features, and certificates.
